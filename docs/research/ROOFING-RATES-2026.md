# Roofing estimate model — calibration against the market, September 2026

**Question from the owner** (on the 917 Littlerock St estimate): *is the amount
of work reflective for this size, the production rate and overall pricing?
Find real invoices and estimates people posted for comparison.*

**Short answer.** The **price** is in range but on the high side for Ottawa;
the **work** was overstated by a quarter, and for one reason that nobody else
in the industry agrees with: the model treated a 6/12 roof as steep. Fixing
that, and bringing the tear-off constant down to what crews actually strip in
an hour, takes 917 Littlerock from **127.7 crew-hours (8.5 days for two)** to
**100.2 crew-hours (6.7 days for two, 3.5 for four)**. The ice & water line
was also short: the prefill laid one 3-ft course at the eave where the
Ontario Building Code, Ottawa practice and every real quote we found lay two.

Three defaults changed. Everything else stays, with a verdict per line below.
Company overrides are untouched — these are the seeds a new company gets and
what the instant estimate uses.

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| 917 Littlerock, 3-tab, all-in sell | **$21,044.54** ($675/sq) | **$19,796.40** ($635/sq) |
| 917 Littlerock, architectural, all-in sell | $26,186 ($840/sq) | $24,470 ($785/sq) |
| Crew-hours (architectural) | 127.7 h — 4.1 h/sq | 100.2 h — 3.2 h/sq |
| Days on site, crew of 2 / 4 | 8.5 / 4.5 | 6.7 / 3.5 |
| Pitch treatment at 6/12 | moderate: ×1.3 labour, +10% sell | walkable: ×1.0, +0% |
| Ice & water prefilled | 231 ft (one course at eave + valleys) | 421 ft (two courses at eave + valleys) |
| Tear-off, one layer | 0.7 h/sq | 0.5 h/sq |

The roof: Google Solar hip, 3,116 sq ft sloped = 31.16 squares, 6/12, 10
facets, footprint 2,778 sq ft. Prefilled linears: drip edge and starter 211 ft
(perimeter), valleys 41 ft, ridge & hip cap 163 ft, ridge vent 51 ft, eave
190 ft. One layer to strip, one storey, no penetrations counted.

### 1.2 Install only

- "About a square of shingles per man hour" — two separate roofers, [Fine Homebuilding](https://www.finehomebuilding.com/forum/attention-roofers-how-many-square-sh) (2007). A pair at 5/12 with paper, drip edge and flashing: ~2 sq/h combined (1.0 h/sq). 12/12 with a nail gun, roofer + helper: 2–3 sq/h (0.7–1.0 h/sq).
- "Today it's rare to get a shingler who can do 8 sq/day — most are 5–6" — [RoofersCoffeeShop, "Production & pay rates"](https://www.rooferscoffeeshop.com/forum/topic/production-pay-rates-for-shingling) (2011). That is 1.3–1.6 h/sq for the shingler alone.
- Piece-rate payroll baseline: **$400/day ÷ 10 sq = $40/sq**, i.e. one installer at 10 sq/day = 0.8 h/sq — [PieceWorkPro](https://pieceworkpro.com/2025/03/04/how-much-to-pay-per-square-in-roofing/) (4 Mar 2025).

**Ours: 1.4 h/sq walkable, ×0.95 for 3-tab, ×1.15 designer.** In range — the
slow end of the shingler figures, which is where a default belongs. **Kept.**

### 1.4 Pitch and storeys

- **Xactimate**: RFG STEEP is **7/12–9/12**, STEEP> 10/12–12/12, STEEP>> over 12/12. A 6/12 is standard labour. [RoofPredict](https://roofpredict.com/blog/how-to-claim-xactimate-steep-slope-charges-correctly), [The Estimate Company](https://theestimatecompany.com/blog/xactimate-line-items-explained/), [Coohom cheat sheet, RFG215 "above 6/12"](https://www.coohom.com/article/ultimate-xactimate-roofing-cheat-sheet) (2025–2026). One 2025 estimate prices the 10/12–12/12 charge at $26.22/sq — [Greenfield IN board packet](https://www.greenfield.in.gov/phocadownload/BOW/Aug-27-2024/girl%20scout%20house%20-%20gladiator%20estimate.pdf) (2024).
- **Piece-rate practice**: standard rate 2/12–6/12; **+15–20% for 7/12–9/12; +40% for 10/12+** — [PieceWorkPro](https://pieceworkpro.com/2025/03/04/how-much-to-pay-per-square-in-roofing/) (Mar 2025).
- Ottawa guides: "10%–25% labour increase (6:12+ pitches)" — [Ottawa Contractors](https://ottawacontractors.ca/costs/roofing-cost-ottawa) (2026); "15–25% labour increase on steep pitches" — [High 5 Exteriors](https://high5exteriors.ca/how-much-does-a-roof-replacement-cost-in-ottawa-2026-guide/) (2026).
- A real GTA quote: "3 feet of ice and water shield at all eaves **6/12 pitch and less**" — 6/12 is the ordinary roof — [RedFlagDeals p.40](https://forums.redflagdeals.com/roofing-questions-shingle-brands-contractors-435222/40/) (Jul 2023).
- Second storey: 15–25% — [RoofVista](https://roofvista.com/resources/guides/second-story-roof-replacement-cost) (2026). Ours: two 1.10, three-plus 1.25. In range. Kept.

**Ours put "moderate" at 6–8/12 (×1.3 labour, +10% sell).** No source draws
the line at 6. Worse, our own public estimator (`steepnessTier()` in
`lib/measure/roofMeasurement.js`) already reads ≤6 as standard and 7–9 as
moderate, so the homeowner's instant price carried no surcharge on a roof the
office builder then surcharged. **Changed: walkable ≤6/12, moderate 7–9/12,
steep 10–12/12, very steep >12/12.** Factors (1.0 / 1.3 / 1.6 / 2.0)
unchanged; 1.3 is the top of the 15–25% band, which is right for a default.
Band keys unchanged, so saved `steepnessSurcharge` overrides still apply.

## 3. Ventilation

- **Rule.** OBC/NBC 9.19.1.2: unobstructed vent area not less than **1/300 of
  the insulated ceiling area**; **1/150** where the slope is less than 1 in 6
  or the roof is built with roof joists; at least 25% of the openings at the
  top and 25% at the bottom. [BC Building Code text of the same NBC clause](https://free.bcpublications.ca/civix/document/id/public/bcbc2018/bcbc_2018dbp9s919), [buildingcode.online](https://www.buildingcode.online/1680.html). IRC R806.2 is 1/150 by default with a 1/300 exception in climate zones 6–8 requiring **40–50% of the area in the upper portion** [up.codes](https://up.codes/s/minimum-vent-area). The ratio applies to the **attic floor**, "not the sloped roof surface" — [Building America Solution Center](https://basc.pnnl.gov/information/calculating-attic-passive-ventilation).
- **Products.** GAF Cobra Rigid Vent 3 / Snow Country Advanced **18 sq in/ft** [GAF data sheet](https://documents.gaf.com/data-sheets/cobra-rigid-vent-3-exhaust-vent-for-roof-ridge-rescb173_data-sheet.pdf); Lomanco OR-4 18 [Lomanco](https://www.lomanco.com/vents/ridge-vents/); OC VentSure strip ~20 [Owens Corning](https://www.owenscorning.com/en-us/roofing/components/products/4-foot-strip-ridge-vent). GAF Master Flow static vent **50 sq in** (60 for the resin one) [Home Depot Canada](https://www.homedepot.ca/produit/gaf-master-flow-event-statique-de-60-pouces-en-resine-de-la-nfa-pour-toit-a-persiennes-carrees-en-brun/1000135675); Lomanco 750 50 [Shell Lumber](https://www.shelllumber.com/lomanco-750-aluminum-roof-louvers.html); Ventilation Maximum 301 (the Quebec staple) **144 sq in** [HVACQuick](https://www.hvacquick.com/products/residential/attic-fans/Attic-Vents/Ventilation-Maximum-301-Series-Sloped-Roof-Vents) — a company laying Maximums edits `NFA_PER_BOX_VENT` or simply divides the count by three.
- **Our arithmetic** (`ventilation()` in `lib/measure/roofGeometry.js`): floor ÷ 300 → × 144 → ÷ 2 → ÷ 18 per ft or ÷ 50 per vent. For 917 Littlerock: **2,778 ÷ 300 = 9.26 sq ft; 667 sq in high; 38 ft of ridge vent or 14 box vents.** That is the standard chain, and the same worked example done independently from the code gives 37.1 ft / 13.3 vents.
- **Is it the wrong area?** No. The panel passes `footprintSqft`, which is Google Solar's `groundAreaMeters2` — the horizontal projection — not the 3,116 sq ft sloped `areaMeters2` ([Solar API reference](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/solar/building-insights): "sloped area 452.00 m², ground projection 443.16 m²"). Using the sloped area would have said 42 ft / 15 vents. The footprint does include the overhangs, so it overstates the insulated ceiling by roughly the overhang band (5–8% on a bungalow); that errs toward one extra box vent, which is the safe direction for a warranty. Unchanged.
- Low slope: ours switches to 1/150 at ≤2/12, which is the code's "less than 1 in 6". Unchanged.

## 4. Waste, bundle math, ice & water, drip edge and starter

### 4.1 Ice & water at the eave — the quantity was short

OBC 9.26.5.1 (Quebec and NBC identical): eave protection "extending from the
edge of the roof a minimum of 900 mm up the roof slope to a line not less
than 300 mm inside the inner face of the exterior wall" — [CodeIndex](https://codeindex.ca/article/9.26.5.1), [qccodes.ca](https://www.qccodes.ca/toiture_et_couverture/protection-des-debords-de-toit-en-bardeaux/), [NRC intent statement](https://codes-guides.nrc.ca/IA/15CNB/intents-intentions/i004378-fra.html). IRC R905.1.2: 24 in horizontally inside the wall line, 36 in along the slope at ≥8/12 [ICC](https://codes.iccsafe.org/s/IRC2021P2/chapter-9-roof-assemblies/IRC2021P2-Pt03-Ch09-SecR905.1.2).

Along-slope length needed = max(35.4 in, (overhang + 5.5 in wall + 12 in) × slope factor):

| Overhang | 4/12 (×1.054) | 6/12 (×1.118) | 8/12 (×1.202) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 in | 35.4 (floor governs) | 35.4 | 35.5 |
| 18 in | 37.4 | **39.7** | 42.7 |
| 24 in | 43.7 | 46.4 | 49.9 |

A single 36-in course only satisfies the code with a 12-in overhang or less.
Two courses give 36 + 32 = 68 in with a 4-in lap. Ottawa–Gatineau roofers say
so in their own words — "the first 6 feet" [Firon Roofing](https://fironroofing.ca/blog/ice-dams-101/), "in most northern regions roofers install two rows (six feet)" [Peace of Mind Exteriors](https://www.peaceofmindexteriors.ca/blog/ice-and-water-shield-roof-protection-installation-guide) — and real quotes read "6 ft of ice and water all around roof including valleys" (Western MA 2025), "6 feet up roof, around vents, skylights, valleys, chimney" (NY 2019). The GTA quote at "3 feet … 6/12 pitch and less" (2023) is the warmer-climate exception; a company that runs one course halves the eave share.

**Change:** `takeoffPatch()` now writes `iceWaterFt = 2 × eave + valley`
(`ICE_WATER_EAVE_COURSES = 2`). The field is feet of 36-in membrane laid, which
is what the $/lf sell, the 0.02 h/lf and the roll count all multiply, so
everything downstream follows: 917 Littlerock goes from 231 ft (4 rolls) to
**421 ft (7 rolls at 200 sq ft, +$294 material, +$665 sell)**. Was 231 ft
consistent with 211 ft of eave? It was 190 ft of eave (the hip rule takes
90% of the perimeter as eave) + 41 ft of valley — internally consistent, one
course, and one course is not what gets laid in Ottawa.

Valley: 36 in centred on the valley is the standard ([build-construct](https://build-construct.com/building/ice-and-water-shield-for-roof-valleys-installation/)); one course, unchanged.

### 4.2 Waste

Gable 5–10%, **hip 15–20%**, complex 20%+ ([OneClickCode](https://www.oneclickcode.com/blog/the-roofing-contractors-guide-to-shingle-waste-factor-calculation), 2026); EagleView's measured suggested-waste factor on a heavily faceted roof was 41% ([EagleView](https://www.eagleview.com/blog/waste-factor-forty-percent-dodecagon-roof/), 2019). Ours is a flat 10% on the rate card — the owner's number, and a quantity factor on the sourcing list, not a price. For 917 Littlerock (10 facets, hip) the yard order is 103 bundles; at 15% it is 108. **Not changed** — it is the company's call and it is one field — but the geometry module already knows the roof's shape, and a follow-up could offer "hip → 15%" as the prefilled waste the way it prefills the linears.

### 4.3 Bundle math (all confirmed against current listings)

- Starter: GAF Pro-Start **120.33 lf** [superarbor](https://superarbor.io/products/gaf-pro-start-starter-strip-shingles-38-pieces-bundle-120-33-lin-ft-coverage); OC Starter Strip Plus **105 lf** [superarbor](https://superarbor.io/products/owens-corning-starter-strip-shingle-plus-black-105-linear-ft-per-bundle); IKO Leading Edge Plus **123 lf** [Beacon](https://www.becn.com/productDetail/C-466535). Ours 120. Correct for GAF.
- Ridge cap: GAF Seal-A-Ridge **25 lf** [Lowe's](https://www.lowes.com/pd/GAF-Seal-A-Ridge-25-lin-ft-Saddlewood-Ranch-Hip-and-Ridge-Roof-Shingles/50383512); TimberTex 20 lf [Home Depot](https://www.homedepot.com/p/GAF-Timbertex-Weathered-Wood-Hip-and-Ridge-Shingles-20-linear-ft-per-Bundle-0840900/100658131); a bundle of 3-tab cut for cap ≈ 35 lf (trade convention). Ours 25. Correct. Hips take the same cap per foot as ridge; the extra on a hip roof is waste, not cap.
- Ridge vent 4-ft sections: correct. 917 Littlerock 51 ft → 15 sections (with 10% waste).
- 33.3 sq ft bundles, three to a square: correct for HDZ.

### 4.4 Drip edge and starter at the whole perimeter

GAF's top wind coverage requires starter "at the eaves and rakes" [GAF warranties](https://www.gaf.com/en-us/resources/warranties/residential); IKO's high-wind warranty: "starter strip shingles must be used at all eaves and rakes" [IKO warranty](https://kent.ca/media/wysiwyg/PDFs/documents/2609200-warranty.pdf). Drip edge at eaves and rakes is standard practice under NBC 9.26.4 flashing rules ([JLC](https://www.jlconline.com/how-to/framing/drip-edge-and-the-irc_o/), [ARCA](https://arcaonline.ca/ss-2/)). On a hip roof the whole perimeter is eave — no rakes — which is what the geometry module does. **211 ft of each on 917 Littlerock is right.** Unchanged.

|---|---|---|---|
| `PITCH_BANDS` walkable / moderate / steep upper bounds | 5 / 8 / 12 | **6 / 9 / 12** | Xactimate, piece-rate practice, GTA quotes and our own `steepnessTier()` all put the first step at 7/12 (§1.4). Factors unchanged; keys unchanged so overrides survive. |

| `takeoffPatch().iceWaterFt` | eave + valley | **2 × eave + valley** | OBC 9.26.5.1 with any overhang over a foot; Ottawa practice; real quotes (§4.1). |
| Everything else | — | unchanged | In range, or no evidence strong enough to move a default (see the verdict columns). |

Files: `lib/pricing/roofLabour.js` (bands, tear-off, the corrected
productivity paragraph), `lib/measure/roofGeometry.js` (`ICE_WATER_EAVE_COURSES`,

**Not changed, and worth a product decision:**

1. **3-tab at $400/sq** stacks to $635/sq all-in on this roof, above Ottawa's
   3-tab guides. The product is being discontinued by BP and no real 2023–2025
   quote was 3-tab. Either drop it from the default seed or accept that a
   company selling it will set its own rate.
2. **Waste by shape.** Prefill 15% for a hip / 20% for a complex roof from the
   geometry the panel already has, instead of a flat 10%.
3. **Cedar at $1,150/sq** is under every Ontario figure; the material cost is
   null by design, so the sell should probably be too until the company sets
   the grade.
4. **Tear-off at $65/sq** is a third of the Ottawa guides' tear-off line. It is
   not wrong in total — the base per-square carries the rest — but a client
   reading the quote sees a cheap tear-off next to an expensive shingle line.
