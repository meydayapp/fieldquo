# Market prices: exterior painting items, cabinet stain vs paint, drywall repairs, ceilings

Researched 3 October 2026 for the owner's ask of the same day. Facts and numbers only, written in our own words; no source text is reproduced. Every page was read on 2026-10-03 unless a row says otherwise.

**How to read this file**

- **Range** means the source publishes a low–high band. **Average** means it publishes one typical figure.
- Currency is **USD** unless the row says **CAD**.
- **[snippet]** means the page refused our fetch (HTTP 403). The figure comes from the search engine's summary of that page and was not confirmed on the page itself.
- **INFERRED** marks a figure we worked out ourselves; the arithmetic is shown beside it.
- Where no Canadian figure existed, a CAD figure is inferred at an **assumed 1 USD = 1.37 CAD**. This rate was not looked up on the day, so treat it as an assumption. HomeStars separately notes that Toronto and Vancouver labour runs 15–25% above the Canadian national average.
- Excluded sources:
  - SEO content farms that showed up in search results (risingsunartscentre, kurlon, steelcase subdomains, contractorplus, usecalcpro and similar).
  - PaintTalk and ContractorTalk: no relevant thread could be reached, either because the index had none or because the page redirected to a paywall.

**INFERRED. The sources split by method:**

- **Re-coating or gel-staining over the existing finish** is cheaper than painting. HomeAdvisor's midpoints, stain $7 and paint $10 per sqft, put staining **30% under** paint (7 ÷ 10 = 0.70).
- **Stripping and re-staining a new colour** costs more than painting. Kitchen Cabinet Kings says +30–50%, but its own door ranges work out to only **+24%** (midpoints 140 ÷ 112.5 = 1.24; kitchens 3,350 ÷ 2,700 = 1.24).
- The WOODWEB figures go the other way (paint over stain), but they are about new cabinets, which is a different job.
- No Canadian staining figure was found.

**TrueFinish's own data** has no cabinet stain price. Its one stain rate is `stainChangePrice` per sq ft on hardwood floors (app/admin/lib/new-services.js, read-only). Nothing in it could be preferred for cabinets.

## What we set, and why

All of these are **defaults**. A company that edits a figure on its rate card keeps its own number, because overrides merge key by key. Existing quotes print from their stored lines; the md5 proof is in `npm run check:estimate-kind-routing` (section 8) and `npm run check:room-presets` (section 7).

### Exterior substrates (lib/pricing/paintTakeoff.js)

- **Format.** These are kept in **hours**, because that is how each substrate is defined: price = hours × the exterior set's $80/h (the owner's recovered rate) + paint at $92.31/gal over 300 sq ft.
- **Why hours.** Hours do not change with currency, so each one is set from published labour time where a source states it. The resulting price is then checked against the ranges above.
- **Provenance.** Each substrate is tagged `market`.

| Substrate | Was | Now | Price it produces | Against |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Exterior door (per side) | 1 h | **1.5 h** | $265.85 a door, both sides | US $165–409 (A1). Labour 1–5 h, about 2 h minimum |
| Window frame (each) | 0.5 h | **1 h** | $87.75 a window | INFERRED $39–88 for 16 lf; HomeAdvisor $100–200 with sashes |
| Trim boards (per lf) | 30 lf/h | **25 lf/h** | $3.51 a linear foot | HomeAdvisor $2 typical; Fixr $1–6; HomeStars CAD 1–3 |
| Shutters (per pair) | 0.9375 h | **2 h** | $167.38 a pair | INFERRED $90–170 a pair (Fixr) |

### Cabinet stain vs paint (app/data/tradePriceBooks.js, cabinet_refinishing)

- **What was added.** A **stain finish add-on** on the refinishing calculator: `addOns.stainFinishPerUnit` = **$45 a door or drawer front**, ticked per job, on top of the unit price.
- **How $45 was reached (INFERRED).** It is the +30% premium for stripping and re-staining (Kitchen Cabinet Kings states +30–50%; its own ranges give +24%), applied to TrueFinish's $150: 0.30 × 150 = 45.
- **Where it shows.**
  - Staining → Cabinets opens with it ticked. Painted cabinets open without it.
  - Refacing has no such add-on.
- **Not priced.** Gel-staining over the existing finish (INFERRED about 15% under paint). That is a different product, and it is left to the owner.

### E2. Stripping to bare wood — hours

No source publishes hours per cabinet door or per stair tread for stripping. These are worked out from per-area figures:

| Source | Date | Region | Figure | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| homewyse — Cost to Remove Paint | 2026-09 | US national | $5.72–11.11 per sq ft: chemical remover, scrape, clean, sand | range |
| homewyse — Cost to Refinish Cabinets | 2026-09 | US national | Prep "up to 6 hr per 100 SF" (scrape and sand for stain) | rate |
| BBS Flooring (Markham ON) — refinish hardwood stairs | 2026-05-11 | GTA, CAD | Sanding a 13-tread staircase 3–5 h; sand & stain CAD 125–145 a tread | range |
| designedcurated.com — strip and restain cabinets | 2026 | US | Stained 28-door kitchen 40–50 labour hours; stripping ~80% of the labour | range (cross-check only) |

**INFERRED, with the arithmetic:**

- Chemical: midpoint $8.42/sq ft, ~80% labour at a $60/h trade rate → ~0.11 h/sq ft. Door ~7 sq ft stripped (two faces of 15" × 30" + edges) → **0.75 h**; drawer front ~2.5 sq ft → **0.25 h**. (designedcurated's figure works out near 1.3 h a door, so 0.75 is the cautious end.)
- Sanding: 6 h per 100 sq ft = 0.06 h/sq ft → door **0.4 h**, drawer front **0.15 h**. Suits a clear finish or one thin coat.
- Stairs, sanding: 3–5 h / 13 treads → **0.3 h a tread**; a riser ~70% of a tread's face → **0.2 h**; handrail ~0.67 sq ft per foot, profiled at twice the flat rate → **0.1 h a foot**.
- Stairs, chemical: the same 0.11 / 0.06 ratio → **0.5 h a tread, 0.35 h a riser, 0.15 h a foot of handrail**.

### What we set (app/data/tradePriceBooks.js, lib/pricing/stainFinish.js)

- Stripping is LABOUR on its own line: hours × `Company.labourSellRate`. Cabinets: when the stain is liquid, or the colour goes lighter. Stairs: only a **painted** staircase stained liquid (or going lighter) — a clear-finished one is sanded by the tread rate already.
- `addOns.stainFinishPerUnit` **45 → 10**: the $45 was the whole strip-and-restain premium (B6); with stripping itemised it would bill the stripping twice. INFERRED remainder: KCK's own ranges +24% = $36 on $150; sanding-level stripping on a 20-door / 8-drawer kitchen is 9.2 h, ~$25 a piece at $75/h; $36 − $25 ≈ $11 → **$10**.
- Not priced: stripping balusters and newel posts (the owner named treads, risers and railing), and the painting book's illustrative staining substrates.
